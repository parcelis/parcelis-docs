# Is There a Cost to Sign Up for TurboTenant?

You can sign up for free, or upgrade to a Parcelis+ subscription based on your portfolio size and needs.

Parcelis is free to sign up and use—there are no upfront costs or required subscriptions to get started.

Most landlords begin on the Free plan and upgrade only if they want additional tools through our Parcelis+ subscription.
