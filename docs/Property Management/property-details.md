Open a property record to review its units, leases, maintenance activity, financial indicators, and contact information.

## Open a property

1. Select a property name in the Portfolio dashboard snapshot table or the Properties directory.
2. Review the summary header and key metrics.
3. Open the relevant section to inspect units, leases, maintenance, financials, or contact details.

## Summary header

The header shows the property name, status, type, address, and an **Edit property** action.

## Key metrics

Property detail pages summarize:

- Total, occupied, and vacant units
- Occupancy percentage
- Monthly rent
- Active leases
- Open tickets
- Leases expiring within 90 days

## Review property data

### Units

The **Units** section shows a sample of units, including whether each is occupied or vacant and the assigned tenant when lease data exists.

### Leases

The **Leases** section shows records attached to the property. A record can include the unit, tenant, start date, end date, status, monthly rent, overdue balance, email, and phone.

### Property contact

When contact information exists, the **Property Contact** section shows the contact name, email, phone, and address.

### Maintenance

The **Maintenance** section lists tickets attached to the property. A ticket can show its title, status, priority, and due date.

### Financials

The **Financials** section summarizes gross scheduled rent, overdue balance, leases expiring within 90 days, estimated vacancy loss, and owner distribution.

<Callout icon="📘" theme="info">
  Unit, lease, maintenance, and financial sections show information when that data exists for the property.
</Callout>
